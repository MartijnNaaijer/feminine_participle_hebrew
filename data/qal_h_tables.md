## Position (Revell 2012): clause end × complement, all 46 forms

Revell (JSS 57, 2012) argues that pausal forms are placed by position (clause end, main division of a clause), not by the accent. A participle followed by its complement cannot end the clause, so the cell with a complement and clause_final = 1 is empty by construction.
